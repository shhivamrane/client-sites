Yes, you can pay in 3 instalments across the year. UPI, card and bank transfer are accepted.

What if my child misses a class?

Every lecture is recorded and available on our app, and the daily doubt hour helps them catch up.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes, all batches have a live online option for students who can't come in every day.
